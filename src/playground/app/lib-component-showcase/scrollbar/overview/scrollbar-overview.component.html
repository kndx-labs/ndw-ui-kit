Touch targets and autohide

On touch-primary devices, scrolling makes the thumb easier to grab for one second. Near the right edge, that temporary target can receive taps that would otherwise reach content underneath it. After it settles, buttons beside the track work normally. Mouse-primary devices keep the scrollbar visible; mouse track clicks move the thumb. Fine-primary hybrid devices allow touch or pen thumb dragging, but have no coarse hit extension or autohide. Hybrid devices with a touch primary pointer keep mouse thumb dragging, but mouse track clicks do not jump the content.

Disabled scrollbar — container grows instead of scrolling

[ndwuScrollbarDisabled] turns the custom scroll box into a pass-through wrapper: both axes grow, both tracks disappear, and the reserved gutter is released. Nothing inside is destroyed by the toggle, so component and DOM state survive — type into the field below, then toggle.

The call site still owns its own height cap. Here scrollbar-grow-demo--grow drops height; without that, the container would stay capped even with the scrollbar disabled.
