The touch target (44px by default) follows the last pointer pressed inside the scroll box: after a touch or pen press it is on, after a mouse press it is off and track clicks work. Autohide follows the primary pointer. The Try it controls set the touch target size and hide delay for both demo boxes below. Check on a real phone or tablet; on desktop Chrome, turn on the DevTools device toolbar and reload.

Try it

  1. Swipe the list. A tinted strip appears around the thumb and disappears about a second after scrolling stops.
  2. Swipe again and, while the strip is visible, drag the thumb. The list follows the finger and the thumb gets wider.
  3. Wait for the strip to disappear, then tap a Ping button at the right edge. Last tap must update: the scrollbar does not take taps at rest.
  4. Turn on ndwuScrollbarAutoHide. The scrollbar is hidden until you scroll. On a touch screen, rows get wider by the freed gutter.
