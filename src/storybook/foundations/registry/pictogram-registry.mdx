# Pictogram Registry

Every pictogram of the kit with its name. Pass the name to `ndwu-pictogram` (`name="pictogram_empty_L"` or
`NDWU_PICTOGRAM_NAME.PICTOGRAM_EMPTY_L`). The suffix sets the size: `_L` 64 px, `_XL` 100 px.

Filter by part of the name or by size. Pictograms show the `DEFAULT` colour preset.
